# Brainstorm Workflow

## Steps

1. **Read the Product Manager skill** — Load `.agents/skills/product-manager/SKILL.md` and follow the Brainstorming Protocol.

2. **Scan current codebase state** — Review `data/changelogData.ts`, `AGENTS.md`, and component structure to understand what exists today.

3. **Frame the problem** — Establish Who / What / When / Why before generating any ideas.

4. **Diverge** — Apply at least 2 brainstorming frameworks (10x Thinking, Inversion, Adjacent User, JTBD) to generate diverse ideas.

5. **Converge** — Score ideas using ICE framework and present as a ranked table.

6. **Deliver** — Output as an artifact with:
   - Problem space framing
   - Ranked ideas table
   - Top recommendation with reasoning
   - Ideas to kill (and why)
   - Concrete next steps
